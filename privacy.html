Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Purpose

This is a personal application used with rclone to synchronize and back up personal files to Google Drive.

2. Information and permissions

The application uses Google OAuth to authorize rclone to access Google Drive through the Google Drive API.

Depending on the permissions granted and the rclone configuration, rclone may read, upload, modify or delete files in Google Drive.

3. Data handling

File transfers are performed directly by the locally installed rclone application between the user's device and Google Drive.

This website does not collect, store or process personal files or Google Drive data.

4. Credentials

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally in the user's rclone configuration. They are not intentionally shared with this website.

5. Third-party services

Google Drive and Google OAuth are subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

6. Contact

For questions about this personal application, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
